JARVIS

A voice-first personal assistant for Mac and Windows. Beta.

Talk to JARVIS in English, Hindi or Hinglish and it helps with everyday work on your computer: open apps, control music and volume, check the weather, manage your calendar, read and draft emails, message and call your contacts, and remember things you ask it to.

First time on a Mac: macOS asks you to confirm the beta once (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway). On Windows, the beta installer isn't signed yet: your browser may warn about the download (choose Keep) and Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” (click More info → Run anyway). All the steps: Mac guide (PDF) · Windows guide (PDF). Download JARVIS only from this page.

You stay in control

  • Anything that sends, changes or deletes something (an email, a message, a calendar event, a call) is shown to you first and only happens after you approve it.
  • JARVIS runs on your computer. Your conversations, contacts and settings are stored on it, and sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS keychain or Windows Credential Manager.
  • You choose which features are on, and can switch any of them off at any time.

Google services

If you choose to connect a Google account, JARVIS uses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and YouTube search only to do what you ask. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and why.
