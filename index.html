JARVIS

A voice-first personal assistant for your Mac. Beta.

Talk to JARVIS in English, Hindi or Hinglish and it helps with everyday work on your Mac: open apps, control music and volume, check the weather, manage your calendar, read and draft emails, message and call your contacts, and remember things you ask it to.

You stay in control

  • Anything that sends, changes or deletes something (an email, a message, a calendar event, a call) is shown to you first and only happens after you approve it.
  • JARVIS runs on your Mac. Your conversations, contacts and settings are stored on your Mac, and sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS keychain.
  • You choose which features are on, and can switch any of them off at any time.

Google services

If you choose to connect a Google account, JARVIS uses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and YouTube search only to do what you ask. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and why.
