Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

JARVIS is a desktop app for macOS made by h4harshill ("we"). This policy explains what data JARVIS uses, where it goes and how you control it. JARVIS is in beta; we will update this page when anything changes.

The short version

  • JARVIS runs on your Mac. We do not run servers that receive your conversations, emails, contacts or calendar. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • To understand and answer you, JARVIS sends your voice, typed requests and the information needed for the answer to Google's Gemini AI, using your own Gemini API key.
  • Nothing is sent, changed or deleted on your behalf without your approval in the app.

What stays on your Mac

  • Conversation transcripts, task history, approvals and an activity log, in a local database in your Mac's user folder.
  • Things you ask JARVIS to remember, routines and settings.
  • A local copy of your Google Contacts (if you connect them), so JARVIS can find people quickly, plus simple counts of whom you message or call most, so it can pick the right person when several share a name. Message content is not kept for this.
  • Sign-in tokens, API keys and passwords you enter are stored only in the macOS keychain. They are never written to logs or diagnostic reports.

Google user data

Connecting a Google account is optional. When you connect one, JARVIS asks for these permissions and uses them only for the features below, only when you ask:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): search and read your emails, show unread mail on the dashboard, create drafts, and send emails or replies that you have approved. JARVIS does not delete emails.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): read your events and create, change or delete events that you have approved.
  • Google Contacts (contacts.readonly): read your contacts to find the right person. JARVIS never changes your Google Contacts.
  • YouTube (youtube.readonly): search for a song you ask to play. Nothing is posted, liked or changed.
  • Your Google email address, to show which account is connected.

Google data is read directly from Google by the app on your Mac. The parts needed to answer a request (for example the text of an email you asked JARVIS to read) are sent to Google's Gemini AI to produce the answer. Google data is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models by us.

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Gemini AI

JARVIS uses Google's Gemini API with an API key you provide. Your audio, typed text, and, when you turn them on, screen images are processed by Google under the Gemini API terms. Please note: on Google's free tier, Google may use content to improve its products; on a paid (billed) key it does not. Choose the plan that fits you.

Other services you can choose to use

  • Brave Search (optional, your own key): only your search query is sent.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): only the city name or coordinates are sent.
  • Other email accounts (e.g. Hostinger, Zoho): JARVIS connects directly to your email provider over an encrypted connection.
  • WhatsApp and calls: JARVIS uses the WhatsApp and Phone/FaceTime apps on your Mac, after your approval.
  • Phone remote (optional): works only on your local Wi-Fi with a paired phone; nothing goes through our servers.

Your choices

  • Turn any feature off in Settings → Connections. Turning off Contacts deletes the local copy.
  • Disconnect a Google account in the app: its access is revoked and its local data is removed. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • View, edit or delete what JARVIS remembers on the Memory page.
  • To remove everything, quit JARVIS, delete the app, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/shilex.

Security

Credentials are kept in the macOS keychain; connections to online services use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Logs and diagnostic reports remove secrets automatically.

Children

JARVIS is not intended for children under 13.
